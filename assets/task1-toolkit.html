Session spine (engaging design)

  1. Beliefs — intentionally more / less; who is constructing meaning?
  2. Unpack a standard — skill · knowledge · conceptual understanding
  3. Technology & AI — Independent or Supported?
  4. Share — what was hardest; what you’ll try next

The clarity test (communicate complexity)

If the tool did this step, could the student still show me the standard?

  • Independent (I): the student does the thinking without the tool doing it for them
  • Supported (S): the tool extends learning; the student still performs the skill in the standard

Desmos turtle reminder: a circle on a distance–time graph “splits” the turtle — one turtle can’t be two places at once. Make the idea visible; don’t lecture the rule.
